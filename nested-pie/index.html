• This chart is a more interactive version of the Region-Country-Language chart. It allows this data to be thought of more as a directory, with languages being put into country "folders" which are then in turn put into region "folders" What you see in the display at any given time is a circle placed within a ring. The inner circle can be thought of as the current folder you're in while the outer ring shows all of the folders in that folder. To navigate to a folder, you can click on that section of the outer ring; to go back you can click on the inner circle.
